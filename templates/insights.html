Key Insights

Findings from the dashboards in this project.

Top Medal Winners

The United States leads with 1,258 medals, followed by China (858) and Russia (502).

Regional Efficiency

North America has the highest medal efficiency (18.51%), then Asia (12.28%). Africa (3.64%) is lowest.

Sport Efficiency

Water Sports has the highest average medals per athlete (0.122), followed by Sliding and Cycling.

Host Nation Advantage

Hosts win more at home: the United States won 198 medals as host and China 115.

Regional Distribution

Europe leads with 2,440 medals, ahead of North America (1,534) and Asia (1,505).

Investment vs Medals

Medal totals rise with sports investment, and the United States is the clear top outlier.
